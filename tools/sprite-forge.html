Sprite Forge

Builds style-locked prompts so every Triad character comes out of your image model as one matched set. The style block is identical for everyone — only the subject changes. Lock one seed and reuse it for the whole batch. Finished art goes to assets/sprites/<id>.png; uncomment the matching line in game.js → SPRITES to swap it in. Full spec in tools/STYLE.md.
